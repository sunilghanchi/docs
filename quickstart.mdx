## Welcome to Biznomate!

This guide will walk you through setting up your first AI-powered campaign in just 3 steps. By the end, you'll have imported your leads, created your first campaign, and started generating results.

## Step 1: Create Your Account

### Sign Up
1. Visit [app.biznomate.com/signup](https://app.biznomate.com/signup)
2. Enter your business email and create a password
3. Choose your plan (Start with our 14-day free trial)
4. Complete your company profile

### Initial Setup
- **Workspace Name**: Choose a name for your team workspace
- **Industry**: Select your industry for better AI recommendations
- **Team Size**: Help us optimize your experience
- **Sales Process**: Tell us about your typical sales cycle

## Step 2: Import Your Leads

### Prepare Your Data
Biznomate accepts leads in CSV format. Your file should include:
- **Email** (required)
- **First Name** (recommended)
- **Last Name** (recommended)
- **Company** (recommended)
- **Job Title** (recommended)
- **Phone** (optional)
- **Custom Fields** (optional)

### Import Process
1. Navigate to **Leads** → **Import**
2. Upload your CSV file
3. Map your columns to Biznomate fields
4. Review and confirm your data
5. AI will automatically score your leads

## Step 3: Create Your First Campaign

### Campaign Setup
1. Go to **Campaigns** → **Create New**
2. Select your target audience (imported leads)
3. Choose a campaign template or create custom
4. Configure your email sequence

### Email Sequence Configuration
- **Subject Lines**: AI will suggest optimized subject lines
- **Email Content**: Use our templates or write custom content
- **Follow-up Schedule**: Set timing for automated follow-ups
- **Personalization**: Add dynamic fields for personalization

### Launch Your Campaign
1. Review your campaign settings
2. Send a test email to yourself
3. Schedule your campaign
4. Monitor performance in real-time

## What Happens Next?

### AI Lead Scoring
- Biznomate's AI analyzes your leads and assigns scores
- High-scoring leads are prioritized for outreach
- Scores update based on engagement and responses

### Automated Follow-ups
- Your campaign automatically sends follow-up emails
- Timing is optimized based on recipient behavior
- Sequences adapt to engagement patterns

### Performance Tracking
- Monitor opens, clicks, and replies in real-time
- Track conversion rates and ROI
- Optimize campaigns based on data insights

## Best Practices

### Lead Quality
- Import clean, verified email addresses
- Include company and job title information
- Segment leads by industry or company size

### Email Content
- Keep subject lines under 50 characters
- Write personalized, value-focused content
- Include clear call-to-actions
- Test different approaches with A/B testing

### Campaign Management
- Start with smaller lists to test
- Monitor and adjust based on performance
- Use AI recommendations for optimization
