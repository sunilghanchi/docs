## Customizable Workflows

Biznomate allows you to tailor the platform to your unique sales process by creating custom lead stages, automation rules, and reporting metrics that match your specific business needs.

## Lead Stage Management

### Custom Lead Stages
Create lead stages that reflect your sales process:

- **Qualification Stages**: New Lead, Qualified, Unqualified
- **Engagement Stages**: Contacted, Responded, Meeting Scheduled
- **Opportunity Stages**: Proposal Sent, Negotiating, Closed Won/Lost
- **Custom Stages**: Industry-specific or company-specific stages

### Stage Configuration
Configure each stage with:

- **Stage Name**: Custom name for your process
- **Stage Description**: Clear definition of what qualifies for this stage
- **Stage Color**: Visual identification in dashboards
- **Stage Order**: Sequential order in your sales process
- **Stage Rules**: Automated criteria for stage progression

### Stage Automation
Set up automatic stage progression based on:

- **Email Engagement**: Opens, clicks, replies
- **Meeting Activity**: Scheduled, completed, cancelled
- **Time-based Rules**: Automatic progression after time periods
- **Manual Updates**: Team member stage changes
- **CRM Sync**: Stage updates from integrated CRMs

## Automation Rules

### Lead Assignment Rules
Automate lead distribution with rules like:

- **Round Robin**: Even distribution across team members
- **Skill-based**: Assign based on expertise or territory
- **Load-based**: Assign to team members with available capacity
- **Score-based**: High-scoring leads to top performers
- **Industry-based**: Assign based on industry expertise

### Campaign Trigger Rules
Automate campaign activation based on:

- **Lead Score Thresholds**: Trigger campaigns for high-scoring leads
- **Stage Changes**: Launch campaigns when leads move to specific stages
- **Engagement Events**: Trigger based on email opens, clicks, replies
- **Time-based Triggers**: Schedule campaigns for specific time periods
- **Custom Criteria**: Trigger based on any lead or company data

### Follow-up Rules
Automate follow-up sequences with:

- **Response-based**: Different sequences for replies vs. no response
- **Engagement-based**: Adjust based on email opens and clicks
- **Time-based**: Follow up after specific time intervals
- **Stage-based**: Different follow-ups for different lead stages
- **Score-based**: Customized follow-ups based on lead score

## Custom Fields and Data

### Custom Lead Fields
Add custom fields to capture your specific data:

- **Text Fields**: Company size, industry, source
- **Number Fields**: Revenue, employee count, budget
- **Date Fields**: Contract renewal, fiscal year end
- **Dropdown Fields**: Product interest, decision timeline
- **Checkbox Fields**: Has budget, decision maker, technical contact

### Custom Company Fields
Track company-specific information:

- **Industry Details**: Sub-industry, technology stack
- **Company Metrics**: Revenue, growth rate, funding status
- **Geographic Data**: Region, time zone, market
- **Relationship Data**: Current customer, partner, competitor

### Data Validation Rules
Ensure data quality with validation rules:

- **Required Fields**: Mandatory information for leads
- **Format Validation**: Email format, phone number format
- **Range Validation**: Numeric value ranges
- **Custom Validation**: Business-specific validation rules

### Automated Reporting
Schedule automated report delivery:

## Integration Workflows

### CRM Integration Rules
Customize how data flows between systems:

- **Field Mapping**: Map custom fields to CRM fields
- **Sync Rules**: Define what data syncs and when
- **Conflict Resolution**: Handle data conflicts between systems
- **Bidirectional Sync**: Two-way data synchronization

### Email Integration Workflows
Configure email automation rules:

- **Send Rules**: When and how emails are sent
- **Reply Handling**: How to process email replies
- **Bounce Management**: Handle undeliverable emails
- **Opt-out Processing**: Manage unsubscribe requests

### Calendar Integration
Automate meeting scheduling:

- **Availability Rules**: Define available time slots
- **Meeting Types**: Different meeting types and durations
- **Follow-up Rules**: Post-meeting automation
- **Calendar Sync**: Integrate with team calendars

## API Reference

### Workflow Endpoints
- **GET /workflows**: List all workflow configurations
- **POST /workflows**: Create new workflow
- **PUT /workflows/{id}**: Update workflow configuration
- **DELETE /workflows/{id}**: Delete workflow

### Webhook Events
- **workflow.triggered**: When automation rules are triggered
- **stage.changed**: When leads change stages
- **automation.completed**: When automation actions complete
- **workflow.error**: When workflow errors occur
