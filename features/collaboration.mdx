## Seamless Team Collaboration

Biznomate enables efficient team collaboration with shared lead lists, campaign templates, and performance reports, helping your sales team hit targets faster with unified workflows.

## Team Workspace

### Shared Environment
Create a unified workspace where your team can:

- **Share Lead Lists**: Collaborate on lead management and scoring
- **Access Campaign Templates**: Use proven templates across the team
- **View Performance Reports**: Track individual and team metrics
- **Coordinate Outreach**: Avoid duplicate contacts and conflicts

### Role-Based Access
Control team member permissions with different access levels:

- **Admin**: Full system access and team management
- **Manager**: Campaign creation, team oversight, reporting
- **Sales Rep**: Lead management, campaign execution, personal metrics
- **Viewer**: Read-only access to campaigns and reports

## API Reference

### Collaboration Endpoints
- **GET /team/members**: List team members and roles
- **POST /team/assign**: Assign leads to team members
- **GET /team/performance**: Team performance metrics
- **POST /team/campaigns**: Share campaigns across team

### Webhook Events
- **lead.assigned**: When leads are assigned to team members
- **campaign.shared**: When campaigns are shared with team
- **performance.updated**: When performance metrics change
- **team.member.added**: When new team members join
