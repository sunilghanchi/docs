## Real-time Performance Analytics

Biznomate provides deep insights into campaign performance with intuitive dashboards, helping you track opens, clicks, replies, and optimize for success with comprehensive reporting.

## Dashboard Overview

### Key Metrics
The main dashboard displays your most important performance indicators:

- **Campaign Performance**: Open rates, click rates, reply rates
- **Lead Quality**: Score distribution, conversion rates
- **Revenue Impact**: ROI tracking, deal pipeline value
- **Team Performance**: Individual and team productivity metrics

### Real-time Updates
All metrics update in real-time, providing instant visibility into:

- **Email Activity**: Opens, clicks, replies as they happen
- **Lead Engagement**: Score changes and behavioral updates
- **Campaign Progress**: Sequence completion and conversion tracking
- **System Health**: Delivery rates and technical performance

## Campaign Analytics

### Performance Metrics

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion icon="envelope-open" title="Email Metrics">
    - **Open Rate**: Percentage of emails opened
    - **Click Rate**: Percentage of emails with link clicks
    - **Reply Rate**: Percentage of emails that received replies
    - **Bounce Rate**: Percentage of undeliverable emails
    - **Unsubscribe Rate**: Percentage of opt-outs
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion icon="chart-line" title="Engagement Metrics">
    - **Time to Open**: Average time from send to open
    - **Click-through Rate**: Clicks per email sent
    - **Response Quality**: Positive vs. negative responses
    - **Meeting Booked**: Conversion to scheduled meetings
    - **Deal Value**: Revenue from campaign-generated opportunities
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion icon="users" title="Audience Metrics">
    - **List Growth**: New leads added over time
    - **Segment Performance**: Performance by lead score, industry, etc.
    - **Geographic Distribution**: Performance by location
    - **Company Size Impact**: Performance by company size
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### Campaign Comparison
Compare multiple campaigns to identify:

- **Best Performing Content**: Subject lines, messaging, timing
- **Optimal Audience Segments**: Which leads respond best
- **Effective Sequences**: Email count and timing optimization
- **ROI by Campaign**: Revenue impact and cost efficiency

## Lead Analytics

### Lead Scoring Insights
- **Score Distribution**: Visual breakdown of lead quality
- **Score Trends**: How scores change over time
- **Conversion Correlation**: Relationship between scores and conversions
- **Score Drivers**: Key factors influencing individual scores

### Lead Behavior Analysis
- **Engagement Patterns**: How leads interact with your content
- **Response Timing**: When leads are most likely to respond
- **Content Preferences**: What types of content drive engagement
- **Journey Mapping**: Lead progression through your funnel

## Revenue Analytics

### ROI Tracking
- **Campaign ROI**: Revenue generated vs. campaign costs
- **Lead Value**: Average revenue per lead by source
- **Conversion Funnel**: Lead-to-customer conversion rates
- **Sales Cycle Impact**: How campaigns affect deal velocity

### Pipeline Analytics
- **Deal Pipeline**: Value of opportunities generated
- **Win Rates**: Percentage of deals that close
- **Average Deal Size**: Revenue per closed deal
- **Sales Velocity**: Time from lead to closed deal

## Team Performance

### Individual Metrics
- **Email Volume**: Number of emails sent per team member
- **Response Rates**: Individual reply rates and quality
- **Meeting Booked**: Meetings scheduled per team member
- **Deal Generation**: Opportunities created per person

### Team Collaboration
- **Shared Performance**: Team-wide metrics and trends
- **Best Practices**: Top performers and their strategies
- **Workload Distribution**: Campaign and lead assignment
- **Training Insights**: Areas for team improvement

- **Revenue Forecasting**: Expected revenue from current campaigns

## API Reference

### Analytics Endpoints
- **GET /analytics/campaigns**: Campaign performance data
- **GET /analytics/leads**: Lead analytics and scoring
- **GET /analytics/revenue**: Revenue and ROI metrics
- **GET /analytics/team**: Team performance data

### Data Formats
- **JSON**: Standard API response format
- **CSV**: Export format for external analysis
- **Webhook**: Real-time data delivery
- **Scheduled**: Automated report delivery
